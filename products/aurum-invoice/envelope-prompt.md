# Aurum envelope: prompts for ChatGPT image generation

Paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then one SHOT at a time. Ask ChatGPT to "keep the exact same envelope, lighting and background in every image".

## STYLE BLOCK (paste this first)

A single luxury invoice envelope, photographed like a high-end jewellery or perfume campaign.

- Envelope: matte black, heavy cotton paper stock, slightly textured, a soft sheen on the edges. Landscape, ratio about 1.45 to 1 (width to height). Crisp, sharp folds. Real paper thickness visible on every edge.
- Flap: classic pointed triangular flap that attaches along the TOP edge of the envelope and points DOWN when closed. Hinged at the top edge.
- Inside of the flap and inside of the envelope: a metallic gold foil lining with a very fine diagonal woven pattern, warm champagne gold (not yellow, not orange).
- Seal: a round wax seal in deep black wax with a glossy, slightly irregular melted edge, pressed with a gold monogram "AC" inside a thin ring. Placed exactly where the point of the flap meets the body.
- Letter: a thick cream card, textured like cotton paper, with a very fine gold edge. Printed with elegant serif lettering (Playfair Display style).
- Background: seamless dark black-brown studio backdrop, no table texture, a very soft warm spotlight behind the envelope, a few tiny gold dust particles floating in the air.
- Lighting: one soft key light from top left, a gentle gold rim light, deep realistic shadow under the envelope. Shallow depth of field.
- Mood: quiet, expensive, ceremonial. Think Cartier, Hermès, Tom Ford.
- Quality: photorealistic, 8k detail, sharp focus on the envelope, no text except where stated, no watermark, no hands, no logos other than the AC monogram.
- Format: square 1:1, envelope centred, with space around it.

## SHOT 1: closed and sealed

Using the style block: the envelope fully closed, flap down, black wax seal with the gold "AC" monogram in the centre where the flap point meets the body. Straight-on view from the front, very slightly from above. Nothing else in the frame.

## SHOT 2: the flap opening (this is the one that matters)

Using the style block: the SAME envelope, seal now broken into two clean halves (the wax cracked along the line of the flap edge). The flap is half open, caught mid-lift at about 60 degrees. The flap is hinged at the top edge of the envelope and is rotating UP and BACK, away from the viewer, exactly like a real envelope flap. The gold lining on the inside of the flap is just starting to show. The top half of the broken seal is still stuck to the flap and lifts with it, the bottom half stays on the envelope. The cream letter is just visible as a thin edge inside. Slight motion feel, a few gold particles rising.

## SHOT 3: fully open, flap standing up

Using the style block: the SAME envelope, flap fully open and folded back past vertical so it leans away from the camera. The point of the flap faces UP and AWAY, with the gold-lined inside surface facing the camera. The flap is attached along the top edge of the envelope body. The gold lined inside of the envelope body is visible as a band along the top. A thick cream letter card is rising out of the envelope about one third of its height, printed with the words "Invoice", "Adaeze Couture" in a serif font and thin grey lines for text. The bottom half of the broken wax seal remains on the envelope front.

## SHOT 4: the letter fully out

Using the style block: the SAME envelope open as in shot 3, with the cream letter card lifted almost entirely out and tilted very slightly towards the camera, a soft glow around the letter, a small gold ribbon or thin gold edge on the card, gold dust drifting upward. The empty envelope with its gold lining stays below.

## If the flap still looks wrong, add this sentence

"Important: the open flap must point AWAY from the envelope, attached along the TOP edge, like a real envelope with its flap opened upward. It must NOT hang down or point into the envelope."

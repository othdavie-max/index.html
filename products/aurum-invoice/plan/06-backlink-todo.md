# 06. Your backlink and mentions to-do list

This file is for you, the owner. A backlink is a link from another website to yours. Google ranks a site higher when trusted sites link to it, and AI assistants recommend products that several independent sites mention. No one can do this part for you, because it needs your accounts and your voice.

**Pace:** two or three items a day. Not all at once. A burst of 40 new links in one day looks unnatural.

## The rules
1. **Never buy links, "SEO packages" or reviews.** It can get the whole site penalised.
2. **Never fake anything:** no fake reviews, no fake addresses, no second accounts praising Aurum.
3. **Say you made it.** When you mention Aurum in a community, say it is yours.
4. **Help first.** In any group or forum, answer real questions for a week before you mention Aurum.
5. **Use the same name and description everywhere** (the texts below). Consistency is how Google and AI assistants know it is all one business.
6. **Do not paste the same post in many places.** Reword it each time.

## Step 8. Partnerships (month 2 onward; the strongest links)
One real partner is worth fifty directories. Send a personal message, not a mass email.
- [ ] **Accountants and bookkeepers** who serve small businesses. Offer the free templates for their clients, and ask whether they would list Aurum among the tools they recommend.
- [ ] **CAC registration agents and business-setup services.** New businesses need invoices on day one. Offer them a "your first invoice" guide to give to clients, with a link.
- [ ] **Brand designers and printers.** They design logos and receipt booklets. Offer the free templates, and suggest Aurum as the digital version.
- [ ] **Business coaches and SME trainers** (Instagram and YouTube). Offer a free guest lesson: "How to invoice so clients pay on time".
- [ ] **SME programmes and hubs** (for example SMEDAN, FATE Foundation, Tony Elumelu Foundation alumni groups, Co-Creation Hub, local business associations). Ask whether their resources page lists free tools and templates.
- [ ] **University entrepreneurship centres.** Offer the templates and the "how to write an invoice" guide for their resources page.
- [ ] **Your happy customers.** Ask each one for an honest review on `/reviews/`, and, if they have a website, whether they would mention the tool they invoice with.
- [ ] **Guest articles** on Nigerian small-business blogs: offer a useful article (not an advert), with one link back in your author line.
- [ ] **Journalist requests:** join Qwoted and Featured (both free) and answer requests about small business, freelancing and getting paid. A published quote usually includes a link.

## What to expect
- Directory listings appear over days to weeks. Their effect on ranking shows over 1 to 3 months.
- Most emails to news sites get no reply. That is normal.
- Ten good links from real sites beat a hundred weak ones. If you only have time for a little, do Steps 1 to 4, then Step 8.

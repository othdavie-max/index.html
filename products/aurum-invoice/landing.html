Questions

Good to know

How do I get my licence key?

It appears on your screen straight after you pay, with buttons to copy it or send it to your WhatsApp. If anything goes wrong, message us on WhatsApp with the email you paid with.

How does my own web address work (Prestige)?

Your clients open your invoices from your own address, like invoice.yourbrand.ng, and they never see the Aurum name. How it works: 1. You buy the name you want from any domain seller (about ₦10,000 to ₦20,000 a year, paid to them, not to us). 2. After you pay for Prestige, send us the name on WhatsApp. 3. We send you one short setting to paste into your domain seller's website, and connect it on our side. 4. Within 24 hours your invoices open from your own address. Until then everything else in Prestige already works.

Is it a monthly subscription?

No. You pay once and it's yours. Updates are included.

Do my clients need to download anything?

No. They open your link in their phone's browser, like any website. No app and no account.

How do my clients pay me?

By transfer to your own bank account (they copy the number in one tap), or by card, transfer or USSD through your own Paystack. The money goes straight to you. Aurum never touches it.

How do I put it on my phone?

After you buy, open Aurum in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone and follow the short steps (or use the Android and iPhone links under the prices). It gets its own icon, opens full screen and works without data.

Which phones does it work on?

Any modern Android phone or iPhone, and computers too. Install it from Chrome on Android, or from Safari on iPhone.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Confirm your email and you can send 3 documents every month for free, using your own name, logo and clients. Free documents carry a small "Made with Aurum Invoice Studio" line and use the classic envelope. Your 3 come back on the 1st of each month. Want unlimited documents, no Aurum line and every opening style? Buy a licence once, no monthly fees.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. The free demo shows exactly what your clients will see, using sample businesses and sample prices. Your own name, logo, clients and amounts unlock when you buy.

Can I share my licence with a friend?

No. Each licence is for one business on its own devices. Your friend can use your referral code to get a discount on their own.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your documents, clients and settings are backed up. Enter your licence key on your new phone and everything comes back. Message us and we'll move your licence across.

Can I get a refund?

Please try the free demo first. If something on our side doesn't work and we can't fix it, you get your money back. Read the refund policy.

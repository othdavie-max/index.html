Questions

Good to know

Do my clients need to download anything?

No. They open your link in their phone's browser, like any website. No app, no account.

Is it a monthly subscription?

No. You pay once for your licence. If you use Paystack, Paystack charges its normal fee on each payment you receive. That's between you and Paystack.

Can I share my licence with a friend?

No. Each licence works on your own devices and is tied to your business name. Your friend can use your referral code to get a discount on their own.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your documents, clients and settings are backed up. Enter your licence on your new phone and everything comes back.

Does it work without data?

Yes. Install it on your home screen and you can write invoices offline. Links and backup catch up when you're back online.

Can my staff use it?

Yes, on your licence's devices. Each device can carry its own name, so documents show who prepared them.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. The free trial has every feature, with a small DEMO ribbon on your documents. Your licence removes it.

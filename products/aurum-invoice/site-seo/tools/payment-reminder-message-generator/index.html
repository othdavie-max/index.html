Which tone to use

Start gentle a few days before the due date. Use "due today" on the day. If a week has passed, say plainly that it is overdue. Keep the final notice for when earlier messages were ignored, and offer a payment plan if the client is struggling.

Questions people ask

Is it rude to remind a customer about payment?

No. A courteous reminder is normal in business, and most customers appreciate it.

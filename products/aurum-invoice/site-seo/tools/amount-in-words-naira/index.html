Write a naira amount in words

Updated 16 November 2026 · 2 min read

Receipts and cheques in Nigeria usually state the amount in words as well as figures. Type the figure and copy the words.

How the words are written

The words follow Nigerian and British style: commas between the groups, "and" before the last part, the naira first, then the kobo, and the word "only" at the end so nothing can be added.

Example: ₦1,250,500.50 is "One million, two hundred and fifty thousand, five hundred naira, fifty kobo only".

Questions people ask

Why write the amount in words?

Words are harder to change than figures, so they protect a receipt or cheque from being altered.

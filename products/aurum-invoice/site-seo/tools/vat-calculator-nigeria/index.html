Nigeria VAT calculator

Updated 16 November 2026 · 2 min read

Type an amount in naira. The calculator adds 7.5% VAT to a price, or works out how much VAT is inside a price that already includes it.

On this page
  1. Calculate
  2. The formulas
  3. A worked example

Calculate



Runs in your browser. This tool works on your phone, needs no account and sends nothing to a server.

The formulas

To add VAT: VAT = price × 0.075, and total = price × 1.075.

To remove VAT: price before VAT = total ÷ 1.075, and VAT = total − price before VAT.

A worked example

₦100,000 plus VAT is ₦107,500, because the VAT is ₦7,500. Going the other way, a total of ₦107,500 contains ₦7,500 VAT, and the price before VAT is ₦100,000.

Please note: Not every business charges VAT, and small businesses may be exempt from charging or filing it. This tool is a calculator, not tax advice. Confirm your own situation with an accountant or the Nigeria Revenue Service.

Questions people ask

What is the VAT rate in Nigeria?

The standard VAT rate in Nigeria is 7.5%. This calculator keeps the rate in one place, so it is easy to update if the law changes.

Do I have to charge VAT on my invoices?

Not every business does. If you are unsure, ask an accountant. In Aurum you can switch VAT on or off for each invoice.

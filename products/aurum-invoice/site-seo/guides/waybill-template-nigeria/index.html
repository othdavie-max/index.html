Waybill in Nigeria: meaning, sample and free template

Updated 16 November 2026 · 3 min read

A waybill is a document that travels with goods. It says what is being sent, who is sending it, who should receive it and who is carrying it. In Nigeria it is also called a delivery note.

On this page
  1. What a waybill is
  2. What to write on a waybill

  4. Waybill, invoice and receipt

What a waybill is

A waybill lists the goods in a delivery. It is not a bill. It does not ask for payment. Its job is to prove what was sent, when, and who received it.

What to write on a waybill

  • Sender: business name, address and phone.
  • Receiver: name, address and phone.
  • Waybill number and dispatch date.
  • Each item and its quantity. Prices are not needed.
  • Rider, driver or vehicle details.
  • A line for the receiver: "Received in good condition", name, signature and date.

Questions people ask

Is a waybill the same as a delivery note?

In Nigeria people use both names for the same document.

Does a waybill need prices?

No. List the items and quantities. Put prices on the invoice.

Who keeps the waybill?

The sender keeps a copy and the receiver signs one. Keep yours so you can prove delivery.

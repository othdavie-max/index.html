Receipt book or digital receipts: which is better?

Updated 16 November 2026 · 3 min read

A receipt book is familiar and costs a few hundred naira. A digital receipt is faster to send and easier to keep. Here is how they compare.

On this page
  1. Paper receipt books
  2. Digital receipts
  3. A sensible middle

Paper receipt books

  • Good: cheap, no phone or data needed, and customers know them.
  • Not so good: they get lost, the carbon copy fades, you cannot search them, and you must be there to write one.

Digital receipts

  • Good: sent in seconds on WhatsApp, always legible, searchable, backed up, and they can carry your logo and a QR code to show they are genuine.
  • Not so good: you need a phone, and some customers still want paper.

A sensible middle

Many businesses give a digital receipt for every sale and print one only when a customer asks. That way you keep a clean record without a pile of booklets.

Questions people ask

Is a digital receipt valid?

A receipt is proof that you received payment. A clear digital receipt with the amount, date, payer and what it was for does that job.

Can I print a digital receipt?

Yes. Download it as a PDF and print it.

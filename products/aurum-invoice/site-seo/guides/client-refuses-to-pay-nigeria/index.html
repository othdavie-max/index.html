What to do when a client will not pay

Updated 16 November 2026 · 4 min read

A late payment is stressful, but most can be recovered with calm, written steps. Here is an order that works, from the first reminder to the small claims court.

Step 1: check your paperwork

Before you chase, make sure you can prove the debt: the invoice or agreement, any messages where the client agreed to the price, proof you delivered, and the due date. Aurum keeps each invoice, shows when the client opened it, and lets you send a receipt for any part payment.

Step 2: remind, politely and in writing

Send a gentle reminder, then one on the due date, then one when it is overdue. Keep them short and always include the invoice link. Use our reminder generator or the examples in the reminder messages guide.

Step 3: speak to them

If messages are ignored, call. Ask what is stopping payment. Often it is cash flow or a mistake, and a calm conversation can fix it.

Step 4: offer a payment plan

If the client cannot pay in full, agree smaller payments on dates, in writing, and issue a receipt for each payment. A part payment that is honoured is better than none.

Step 5: send a final notice

Write a firm, polite final notice with the amount, the date you expect payment, and what you will do next. Keep a copy.

Step 6: consider the small claims route

Reports say Lagos has a small claims court for debts up to ₦5 million, designed so people can bring a simple claim without a lawyer, starting with a complaint form at the court registry of the magisterial district and often a mediation step. Check the current limit, fees and forms with the court registry. A debt on a simple contract also has a time limit for filing, reported as six years in Lagos, so do not wait.

Questions people ask

Should I stop work if a client has not paid?

If your agreement says you can pause work for non-payment, yes. Say so calmly and in writing, and offer to continue as soon as the payment arrives.

Do I need a lawyer to use the small claims court?

Reports say the process is designed so people can represent themselves. Ask the court registry what you need.

Is a WhatsApp message enough as proof?

Messages where the client agrees to the price and delivery help a lot. Keep them, together with your invoice and proof of delivery.

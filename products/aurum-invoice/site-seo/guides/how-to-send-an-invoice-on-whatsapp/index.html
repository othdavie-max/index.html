How to send an invoice on WhatsApp the professional way

Updated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

Most Nigerian businesses send invoices on WhatsApp, but how you send it changes how it is received. Here are the options, a message you can copy, and what to avoid.

On this page
  1. Three ways to send an invoice on WhatsApp
  2. What to avoid
  3. A message you can copy
  4. Tips so it gets read
  5. Want to know when they open it?

Three ways to send an invoice on WhatsApp

  1. A typed message. Quick, but looks informal, easy to lose and hard to read for long lists. Best only for very small, one-off sales.
  2. A PDF or image. Looks like a real document and can be saved. Make sure it is clear on a phone screen.
  3. A private link. The customer taps a link and sees the invoice on a clean page, often with a way to pay. You can see when it has been opened.

What to avoid

  • Long lists of prices typed in a chat.
  • A blurry screenshot of a spreadsheet.
  • Putting account details in a message and nothing else. The invoice should contain them too.
  • Sending without a due date.

A message you can copy

WhatsApp messageHello [Name], thank you for your order! 🙏 Please find invoice [INV-0042] for ₦[amount] attached [or: here: link]. Payment details are on the invoice. Kindly send proof of payment once done. Thank you for your business.

Tips so it gets read

  • Keep your message short and put the details in the document.
  • Send the invoice in the morning, not late at night.
  • Send it right after the order is agreed.
  • Save the customer to your contacts first, so your message is not marked unknown.

Want to know when they open it?

Questions people ask

Is it okay to send an invoice on WhatsApp?

Yes. It is the normal way for many Nigerian businesses. Use a clear PDF or a private link, and keep the details on the invoice itself.

Should I send a PDF or an image?

PDF is best for a document that can be saved and printed. An image is fine for quick viewing. Make sure either is readable on a phone.

How do I know if my customer saw the invoice?

WhatsApp read ticks only show the message was read, not the invoice. A private link shows when the invoice itself was opened.

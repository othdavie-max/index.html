Car sales receipt in Nigeria: sample and what to include

Updated 16 November 2026 · 4 min read

When a car is sold, a receipt records the payment. It is useful, and you should always have one, but it is not the same as changing the ownership of the car.

On this page
  1. What a car sales receipt should show
  2. A car sales receipt sample
  3. A receipt is not proof of ownership
  4. Sources

What a car sales receipt should show

  • The date, and the names, addresses and phone numbers of the seller and the buyer.
  • The car: make, model, year, colour, plate number, chassis number and engine number.
  • The amount paid, in figures and in words, and how it was paid.
  • Any balance still owed, and when it is due.
  • The condition it was sold in, for example "sold as seen".
  • Signatures of both people, and two signed copies, one for each.

A receipt is not proof of ownership

A receipt records that money was paid. Ownership of a vehicle is changed through the vehicle licensing process and the records kept by the police Central Motor Registry. The seller and buyer should complete the change of ownership, and the buyer should check the proof of ownership papers, making sure the chassis and engine numbers match the car, before paying in full.

Steps and fees change, so confirm the current process with your state vehicle licensing office or the Federal Road Safety Corps.

Please note: A receipt records the payment. Change of ownership and the vehicle papers are separate and must be done properly. This guide is general information, not legal or tax advice. Confirm your own situation with an accountant or lawyer.

Questions people ask

Do I need a receipt if I paid by bank transfer?

Yes. The transfer shows money moved. The receipt says what it was for and records the car, so keep both.

Can the seller keep the car papers until I pay in full?

That is a common arrangement. Agree it in writing, and complete the change of ownership as soon as the final payment is made.

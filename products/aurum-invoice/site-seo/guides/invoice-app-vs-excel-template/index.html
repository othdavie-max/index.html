Invoice app or Excel template: which should you use?

Updated 16 November 2026 · 3 min read

Both work. The right choice depends on how many documents you send and how much time you want to spend on them.

On this page
  1. When a template is enough
  2. When an app starts to help
  3. Where the risks are

When a template is enough

If you send one or two invoices a month, a free Excel or Word template is enough. You fill it in, save it as a PDF and send it. It costs nothing and you control it. There are free invoice and receipt templates on our guides that you can download.

When an app starts to help

  • You send many documents. An app remembers your customers and prices, so you stop retyping.
  • You want to know it arrived. A private link tells you when the client opens it.
  • You chase payments. An app shows who still owes you and helps you remind them.
  • You use your phone. Most spreadsheets are awkward on a phone.

Where the risks are

With a template, the risk is mistakes and lost files: a wrong total, a copied number or a document nobody can find later. With an app, the risk is depending on a service. Choose one that lets you export your records.

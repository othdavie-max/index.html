House rent receipt sample for Nigeria

Updated 16 November 2026 · 3 min read

A rent receipt is the tenant's proof that they paid. For a landlord or agent it is also the record that stops arguments about what was paid and for which period.

On this page
  1. What a rent receipt must show
  2. A rent receipt sample
  3. Keep it separate from other payments

What a rent receipt must show

  • The tenant's name and the property address.
  • The amount paid, in figures and in words.
  • The period the rent covers, for example 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2027.
  • How it was paid: transfer, cash or cheque, and the date.
  • Who received it, with a name and signature.

A rent receipt sample

Receipt R-0031

Received from Mr Bello Adeyemi the sum of ₦1,800,000 (One million, eight hundred thousand naira only), being rent for Flat 3, 14 Allen Avenue, Ikeja for the period 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2027.

Paid by bank transfer on 12 December 2026.
Received by: ______________ (Landlord / Agent)

Keep it separate from other payments

If the tenant also pays a caution deposit, agency fee or service charge, give each its own line or its own receipt. A single mixed amount is the usual cause of disagreements later.

Questions people ask

Should I give a receipt for a bank transfer?

Yes. A transfer alert shows money moved, but your receipt says what it was for and for which period.

Withholding tax on invoices in Nigeria, explained simply

Updated 16 November 2026 · 4 min read

Sometimes a company pays you less than your invoice and says the difference is withholding tax. Here is what that means, and what you should keep so you do not lose that money.

On this page
  1. What withholding tax is
  2. Why the payment can be less than the invoice
  3. Small businesses and small amounts
  4. What to ask for and keep
  5. How to show it in your records
  6. Sources

What withholding tax is

Withholding tax (often shortened to WHT) is tax that the person paying you takes out of the payment and sends to the tax authority on your behalf. It is not an extra charge. It is an advance payment of your own tax, which you can use as a credit when your tax is worked out.

Why the payment can be less than the invoice

If a company deducts WHT, it pays you the invoice amount less the tax it deducted. For example, if your invoice is ₦500,000 and the deduction is ₦25,000, you receive ₦475,000. The rate depends on the type of work or transaction, so it is set out in the withholding tax regulations rather than chosen by the company.

Small businesses and small amounts

Tax firms report that the 2024 withholding tax regulations exempt some small transactions: where the supplier is a small business, has a valid tax identification number (TIN), and the amount in the month is within a stated limit. Reports describe the limit as ₦2 million a month, but the sources differ on the exact start date and conditions, so read the gazetted regulations or ask an accountant before you assume you are exempt.

What to ask for and keep

  • The WHT credit note (or evidence of deduction) from the company, for every deduction. It is your proof that tax was paid for you.
  • Your invoice, showing the full amount, and a note on it that WHT may apply.
  • The payment record, showing what you actually received.

Without the credit note, you may not be able to claim the tax that was deducted.

How to show it in your records

Record the full invoice as income, and record the WHT as tax already paid. In Aurum you can add the deduction in the note on the receipt, so your records and the company's agree.

Sources

  • EY: Nigeria issues Deduction of Tax at Source (Withholding) Regulations 2024
  • Forvis Mazars: Simplified and business-friendly WHT regulations 2024
  • The gazetted Regulations (hosted by KPMG)

Checked on 16 November 2026. Rules change, so confirm the current position before you rely on a figure or a date.

Questions people ask

Is withholding tax an extra cost to me?

No. It is part of your own tax, paid early on your behalf. Keep the credit note so you can use it.

Can I refuse a deduction?

Not if the law requires the company to make it. If you think the deduction is wrong, ask for the reason in writing and speak to an accountant.

Should I put WHT on my invoice?

Show the full amount on your invoice. You can add a line saying that withholding tax may be deducted and that you will need the credit note.

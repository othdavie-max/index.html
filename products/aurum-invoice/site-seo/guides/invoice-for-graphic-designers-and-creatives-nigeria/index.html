How graphic designers and creatives in Nigeria should invoice

Updated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

Creative work is easy to argue about, because "one more small change" costs real time. A good invoice records the scope, the number of revisions and when the client gets the final files.

On this page

  2. A sample invoice
  3. Deposit and payment terms
  4. Problems a clear invoice prevents
  5. Make it quick and look premium

What to put on the invoice

  • The deliverables: logo, flyers, social pack, video edit, with sizes or formats.
  • Number of concepts and revisions included. After that, each round costs extra.
  • Turnaround time and the date the draft is due.
  • File ownership: when the client gets the full-resolution and source files.
  • Extras: rush fee, stock photos, fonts, printing.

Deposit and payment terms

Take 50% before you start and the balance before sending final files. Say clearly that source files are released on full payment. If the client asks for more revisions than agreed, send a short invoice for the extra round, so it never feels like an argument. Our quotation template helps you agree the scope first.

Problems a clear invoice prevents

  • Endless revisions. The invoice states how many are included.
  • Files used without payment. They are only released after the balance is paid.
  • Scope disputes. The deliverables are listed in writing.

Questions people ask

How many revisions should I include?

Two or three rounds is common. After that, charge per round.

When should I release the final files?

After the full payment is confirmed in your own bank account.

Should I invoice before or after the work?

Take a deposit before you start, and invoice the balance when the draft is approved or before the final files go out.

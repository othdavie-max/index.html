How interior designers in Nigeria should write an invoice

Updated 16 November 2026 · 2 min read

Interior jobs run for weeks and change as they go. Split the money into stages and record every change in writing.

On this page
  1. What to put on the invoice
  2. A sample invoice
  3. Make it quick and look premium

What to put on the invoice

  • The design fee as its own line.
  • Materials and labour separated, so the client sees where the money goes.
  • Stage payments: for example deposit, mid-way and handover.
  • A variation note, with a new amount, whenever the client adds or changes something.

Questions people ask

Should I take a deposit?

For work that costs you money up front or cannot be resold, yes. State the deposit and the balance due date on the invoice.

Should I send a quotation first?

For larger or changing jobs, yes. A quotation agrees the price before work starts, and you turn it into an invoice when the customer accepts.

Invoicing for Instagram and WhatsApp sellers in Nigeria

Updated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

Selling through DMs is fast, but it is also messy. Prices get typed into chats, orders get lost, and customers doubt whether you are real. A simple invoicing habit fixes all three and makes you look like a brand, not just a page.

On this page
  1. Why DM sellers need invoices
  2. What to send, and when
  3. A simple order flow
  4. Protect yourself
  5. Make it feel premium

Why DM sellers need invoices

  • Trust. New customers are cautious about paying strangers online. A proper invoice with your business name and details reassures them.
  • Fewer disputes. Everything agreed is written in one place: item, price, delivery fee, date.
  • Fewer lost orders. A chat scrolls away. An invoice is a record you can find.
  • A better look. Compare "Perfume 5k, delivery 1500, total 6500" typed in a chat with a clean, branded document. Which one would you trust with your money?

What to send, and when

  1. Quotation (optional): when a customer asks "how much is this?" and the order is not simple.
  2. Invoice: once they decide to buy, with items, delivery fee, total and your account details.
  3. Receipt: once you confirm the money in your bank app.
  4. Delivery note (optional): for larger orders, to confirm what was sent. See delivery note vs invoice.

A simple order flow

Order confirmationThank you for your order, [Name]! 🎉 Here is your invoice [INV-0012] for ₦[total]. Please pay to [Bank · Account name · Account number] and send the proof here. Your order ships once I confirm payment. Delivery: [x] days.

Protect yourself

  • Check payment in your own bank app, never from a screenshot alone. Fake alerts exist.
  • Ask for payment before you ship, or use a deposit for custom orders.
  • Keep your own list of every order and who has paid.

Make it feel premium

Add your logo, use consistent colours, and put a short thank-you line at the end. Little details make a small brand feel established. Questions people ask

Do I need an invoice if the customer pays on the spot?

It still helps. An invoice or receipt gives both of you a record, and it makes your business look professional.

What should a DM seller put on an invoice?

Your business name, the customer's name, each item with its price, the delivery fee, the total, your account details and a payment deadline.

How do I stop fake payment screenshots?

Confirm every payment in your own bank app or statement before releasing goods.

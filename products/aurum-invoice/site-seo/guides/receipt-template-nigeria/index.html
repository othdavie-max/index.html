Receipt template for Nigerian businesses

Updated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

A receipt is proof that a customer paid you. It protects both sides: the customer can show they paid, and you have a record of what you received. Here is a clear template and what it must contain.

The template

RECEIPT

[Your business name]
[Address] · [Phone] · [Email]

Receipt no: RCT-0001   Date: [DD/MM/YYYY]

Received from: [Customer name]

Amount received: ₦[ ] ([amount in words])

Payment for: [Invoice number or description]

Payment method: [Bank transfer / Cash / POS / Card]   Reference: [transfer reference, optional]

Balance remaining: ₦[ ] (or "Paid in full")

Signed: ____________   Thank you.

What a receipt must show

  • The word Receipt and a receipt number.
  • The date you received the money.
  • Who paid and who received it.
  • The exact amount, ideally also in words, to avoid arguments.
  • What the payment was for, such as an invoice number.
  • The payment method, and the transfer reference if it was a bank transfer.
  • Any balance still owed, if the customer only paid part.

Part payments and deposits

If a customer pays a deposit, issue a receipt for the deposit and show the balance still due. When they pay the rest, issue a second receipt. Each receipt should refer to the same invoice number so the record is easy to follow. We cover this in deposits and part payments.

Check the money before you issue the receipt

Fake transfer alerts and edited bank screenshots are a real problem for sellers in Nigeria. Always confirm the money in your own bank app or account statement, not from a screenshot the customer sends. Issue the receipt only after you see the funds.

Questions people ask

Is a receipt the same as an invoice?

No. An invoice asks for payment. A receipt confirms payment was received. See our guide on invoice vs receipt vs quotation.

Do I have to give a receipt for every payment?

It is good practice to give one, and customers and companies often ask for it. Ask your accountant about requirements for your business.

Can I issue a receipt for a bank transfer?

Yes. Include the transfer reference and the date. Only issue it after you have confirmed the money in your own account.

How to choose the best invoice app for a small business in Nigeria

Updated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

There is no single "best" invoice app. The best one is the one you will actually use every week. Here is what to check before you pay for anything, the main types of tools compared fairly, and where Aurum fits and where it does not.

8 things to check before you choose

  1. Does it work on your phone? Most Nigerian small businesses invoice from a phone, often with poor data. Check it is easy on a small screen and works offline.
  2. Can you add your logo, signature and bank details once? They should appear on every document automatically.
  3. Does it make all the documents you need? Invoices, receipts and quotations at least. Delivery notes, credit notes and purchase orders help if you supply companies.
  4. How does your customer receive it? A PDF, an image, a link? Some tools also tell you when the customer opened it.
  5. Can you track who has paid? You need to see unpaid invoices and send reminders without retyping.
  6. What does it cost over a year? Compare a monthly fee with a one-time payment. Monthly fees in dollars can hurt when the naira moves.
  7. Is it made for Nigeria? Naira amounts, Nigerian banks, a field for your CAC number and TIN, and VAT lines.
  8. Can you try it first, and what is the refund rule? A good seller lets you see it before you pay.

The main types of tool, compared fairly

  • Word or Excel templates. Free and familiar. Good for a few invoices a month. Slow once you send many: you retype details, renumber by hand and have no automatic tracking.
  • Free online invoice generators. Quick for one-off invoices. Often add the tool's own branding, may not save your customers, and rarely track payments or send reminders.
  • Full accounting software. Covers invoices plus bookkeeping, payroll, stock and tax reports. Powerful, but often built around a desktop and a monthly subscription. It can be more than a small seller needs.
  • Phone-first invoice apps. Built for sending documents quickly from your phone and following up on payment. Usually simpler, and cheaper than full accounting software.

Who each option suits

  • Just starting, a few invoices a month: a free template is fine.
  • Selling on WhatsApp and Instagram, several invoices a week: a phone-first app saves real time and makes you look more established.
  • Registered company with staff, stock and payroll: look at full accounting software, possibly alongside an invoicing app.

Questions to ask before you pay

  • Can I see a demo with sample data first?
  • Is it a one-time payment or a subscription, and in which currency?
  • What happens to my data if I change phones?
  • How many devices can I use it on?
  • What is the refund policy, in writing?

Questions people ask

What is the best invoice app in Nigeria?

It depends on your business. For a small seller who invoices from a phone, look for something that works offline, uses naira and Nigerian banks, makes receipts and quotations, and does not charge a monthly fee.

Is there a free invoice app for Nigeria?

There are free templates and free online generators, which are fine for a few invoices. Free tools often lack payment tracking, reminders and your own branding.

Do I need accounting software to send invoices?

No. Many small businesses only need an invoicing tool at first. Add accounting software when you need payroll, stock control or detailed reports.

Is Aurum accounting software?

No. Aurum is an invoicing app. It does not run payroll, track stock or file taxes.

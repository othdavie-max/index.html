Proforma invoice in Nigeria: what it is and when to use it

Updated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

A proforma invoice is a preliminary invoice you send before goods are delivered or work is done. It lets the customer see the cost, arrange payment or documents, and confirm the order.

What a proforma invoice means

"Pro forma" means "for the sake of form". It looks like an invoice, but it is an advance document, a statement of what you intend to charge. It is usually not your final request for payment. After the sale is complete you issue the real invoice.

When to use one

  • When a customer needs a price document to raise funds or get approval before paying.
  • When you are quoting for imports or exports, where customs or banks ask for one.
  • When you want to confirm an order and price before you buy stock or start production.
  • When you take a deposit before the full invoice is ready.

Proforma invoice vs quotation vs invoice

  • Quotation: a price offer. The customer may or may not accept.
  • Proforma invoice: a pre-sale invoice, usually after the customer has shown clear intent to buy.
  • Invoice: the final request for payment after delivery or completion.

This is a proforma invoice. It is not a demand for payment and is not a tax invoice.

Things to get right

  • Mark it clearly as Proforma so no one mistakes it for a final invoice.
  • Give it its own numbering, such as PRO-0001.
  • State how long the prices are valid.
  • Do not count it as income until you issue the real invoice and are paid. Ask your accountant how to record it.

Questions people ask

Is a proforma invoice legally binding?

Usually not on its own. It is a statement of expected charges. A signed order or accepted quotation carries more weight. Ask a lawyer about specific contracts.

Can a customer pay against a proforma invoice?

Yes, many do, especially for deposits. Issue a receipt when the money arrives, and a final invoice when the sale is complete.

Is a proforma invoice the same as a quotation?

They are similar, but a proforma looks like an invoice and is usually sent once the customer has decided to buy.

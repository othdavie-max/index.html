School fees receipt sample for Nigerian schools

Updated 16 November 2026 · 3 min read

Parents pay school fees in parts, often by transfer. A clear receipt for each payment saves the bursar and the parent from arguments later.

On this page
  1. What a school fees receipt should show
  2. A school fees receipt sample
  3. Issue them in seconds

What a school fees receipt should show

  • The school's name and the receipt number.
  • The pupil's name and class, and the term and session.
  • The amount paid, in figures and in words, the date and how it was paid.
  • What it was for: tuition, books, uniform, transport.
  • The balance still owing, if any, and who received the payment.

Questions people ask

Should I give a receipt for part payments?

Yes. Give one for every payment and show the balance, so both sides always agree.

Can parents verify a receipt?

Aurum receipts can carry a QR code that confirms the receipt came from the school.

What is wrong with typing prices in a chat?

  • It looks informal, and easy to doubt.
  • It gets lost in the scroll.
  • There is no proper record for either of you.
  • You cannot tell if the customer actually saw it.

How the private link works

  1. You make the invoice in Aurum.
  2. You pick how it opens: a wax-sealed envelope, a gift box or an invitation card.
  3. You send the link on WhatsApp.
  4. Your client taps it, breaks the seal, and sees exactly what to pay.
  5. You see the moment they open it. No more "did you see my message?".

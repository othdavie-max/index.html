A proforma invoice maker for Nigerian businesses

Updated 16 November 2026 · 3 min read

A proforma invoice shows a customer exactly what the final invoice will say, before you supply anything. In Aurum you make an invoice, set its title to "Proforma Invoice", and send it as a private link or a PDF. When the customer agrees, you issue the real invoice and, later, the receipt.

On this page
  1. What goes on a proforma invoice
  2. How to make one in Aurum
  3. From proforma to invoice to receipt

What goes on a proforma invoice

  • Your business name, logo and contact details, and the customer's.
  • Each item with its quantity and price, plus delivery and VAT if you charge them.
  • The title "Proforma Invoice", so nobody mistakes it for a bill to pay yet.
  • How long the prices are held, and how to pay the deposit if one is needed.

How to make one in Aurum

  1. Open Aurum and tap New invoice.
  2. Add your customer and your items, as for any invoice.
  3. On the last step, open Customise and choose the title Proforma Invoice.
  4. Create the private link, or download the PDF, and send it on WhatsApp.
  5. When the customer agrees, make the final invoice from it and send that.

From proforma to invoice to receipt

A proforma is a preview. The invoice is the request for payment, and the receipt is the proof that you were paid. Keeping the three separate keeps your records clean, and customers who need a proforma to arrange payment or clear goods get exactly what they asked for.

Questions people ask

Is a proforma invoice the same as a quotation?

They are close. A quotation is an offer of a price. A proforma invoice looks like the final invoice and is often asked for by a customer's finance team, or to arrange payment. Aurum makes both.

Can a proforma invoice be used to pay?

A customer can pay a deposit against it if you ask for one, but the invoice you issue afterwards is the document that records what is owed.

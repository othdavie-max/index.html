A waybill and delivery note maker for businesses that deliver

Updated 16 November 2026 · 3 min read

A waybill, also called a delivery note, lists what you sent, who is carrying it and who should receive it. With Aurum you make one on your phone in a minute, or start it from an invoice in one tap, and the customer signs that the goods arrived in good condition.

What goes on a waybill

  • Your business: name, logo, address and phone number.
  • The receiver: name, address and phone number.
  • The goods: each item with its quantity. A waybill does not need prices.
  • Who is carrying it: the rider, driver or vehicle, and the dispatch date.
  • Proof of receipt: a line for the receiver to confirm the goods arrived in good condition, with a name, signature and date.

Start from an invoice in one tap

If you already made an invoice for the order, you do not retype anything. In Aurum, open the invoice and make a delivery note from it. The items and the customer are copied across, and you add the rider and the dispatch date.

You can send it as a private link, a PDF or an image, the same way as an invoice.

Proof of delivery

Most delivery disputes are about one thing: did it arrive, and was it in good condition? A signed waybill answers both. Keep a copy of every one. When a customer says an item never came, you can show the date, the rider and the signature.

Who uses it

  • Online sellers who send orders by dispatch rider or park.
  • Logistics and dispatch businesses that carry goods for other people.
  • Wholesalers and distributors who deliver in bulk and want the shop owner to sign.

Questions people ask

Is a waybill the same as a delivery note?

In Nigeria the two names are often used for the same document: a list of goods being delivered. Aurum calls it a delivery note and lets you title it Waybill.

Does a delivery note need prices?

No. It lists items and quantities. Prices belong on the invoice.

Can the customer sign on their phone?

A delivery note has a printed line for the receiver's name and signature. You can send it as a PDF or image to print, or collect the signature on paper and keep a photo.

Why an invoice app instead of Word or Excel?

Templates are fine for a few invoices. As you grow, the retyping, renumbering and chasing eats your time. An invoice app fills in your details automatically, remembers your customers and prices, keeps a record of everything, and helps you follow up on late payment.

It also changes how your business looks. A clean, branded document, delivered as a private link, signals that you are organised and serious, which helps customers trust you and pay faster.

What you get with Aurum

How it works

  1. Set up your business once: logo, name, bank details, signature.
  2. Make an invoice in under a minute: pick or type the client, add what you sold, choose how it opens.
  3. Send the private link on WhatsApp.
  4. See when it is opened, then turn it into a receipt in one tap when you are paid.

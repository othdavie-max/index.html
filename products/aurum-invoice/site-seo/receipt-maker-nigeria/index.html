Why customers want a proper receipt

Customers and companies keep receipts as proof for their own records, for returns, and for accounts. A clean, branded receipt builds trust and gets saved, instead of a screenshot in a chat that disappears.

How receipts work in Aurum

  1. Open the invoice that was paid.
  2. Tap Issue receipt and confirm the amount and payment method.
  3. The receipt is made with your logo, signature and a Paid mark, and the invoice balance updates itself.
  4. Send it as a PDF, an image or a private link.

Part payments and deposits

If a customer pays in stages, issue a receipt for each payment. Aurum shows what is paid and what is left, so the numbers always agree. See deposits and part payments.

A thank-you they will want to screenshot

When a client pays, they can also receive a thank-you card from your business. It is a small touch that makes people remember you.

What a receipt should contain

Receipt number, date, who paid, the amount (also in words), what it was for, the payment method and any balance left. Full checklist in our receipt template guide.

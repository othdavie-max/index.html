About Aurum Invoice Studio

Aurum Invoice Studio is an invoicing app made in Nigeria, for Nigerian businesses. It exists because too many good businesses send customers a typed list of prices in a chat, and deserve to look as good as their work.

What we make

Aurum makes invoices, receipts, quotations, delivery notes, credit notes and purchase orders, and delivers them as a private link your client opens as an experience. It installs on your phone like an app and works without data. You pay once and there are no monthly fees. See what is inside.

How buying works

  1. Pick a plan on the pricing section and pay securely on Paystack by card, bank transfer or USSD. Paystack handles the payment, so we never see or store your card details.
  2. Your licence key appears on your screen straight after payment, with a button to send it to yourself on WhatsApp.
  3. Install the app on your phone, enter the key, and set up your business.

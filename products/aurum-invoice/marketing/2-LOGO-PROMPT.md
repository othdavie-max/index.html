# Aurum logo: prompt for ChatGPT image generation

## What's wrong with the current logo (so the new one fixes it)
The current mark is a gold double ring around "Au" on black. It's tidy, but:
- **"Au" is the chemical symbol for gold.** It doesn't say Aurum, and it doesn't say invoices.
- It's a **generic circle-with-initials**. Thousands of brands look like it.
- Nothing in it connects to your product's best feature, the **wax-sealed envelope**.

The new logo should look like a **wax seal with an elegant "A"**. It's instantly recognisable, ties to the envelope, and works at app-icon size.

## How to use
1. Open a **new ChatGPT chat**. Attach `ref-current-logo.png` and `ref-wax-seal.png` (and your envelope photo if you like).
2. Paste the **MASTER PROMPT** below.
3. Ask: *"Give me 4 different variations."* Pick one, then ask for the deliverables list below one by one.
4. If a letter or word comes out wrong, ask for the **symbol without any text**, and add "AURUM" yourself in Canva with the font **Playfair Display** (letter-spaced). That's the reliable way to get spelling right.
5. Send me the final PNG files. I'll put them into the app icon, the installed-app icon, the sales page and the browser tab.

## MASTER PROMPT (copy everything in the box)

> Design a professional **brand logo** for **"AURUM Invoice Studio"**, a premium invoicing app for Nigerian businesses. Its signature feature is that invoices are delivered to clients inside a **wax-sealed envelope** that opens like a gift. The brand feels luxurious, calm, trustworthy and ceremonial, in the spirit of Cartier, Hermès and Chanel: simple, timeless, expensive.
>
> **Concept: a refined wax-seal monogram.** A perfectly round seal with a softly **scalloped edge** (12 to 14 gentle, even lobes, like pressed wax). In the centre, a bold, elegant capital **"A"** in a high-contrast Didone / Playfair-Display-style serif. Make the A subtly connect to the envelope idea: the crossbar of the A can be formed by a thin **envelope-flap "V" line** or a slim horizontal bar, so that it quietly reads as an A and as a flap opening. Add one tiny gold spark or small star above the crossbar. A thin inner ring sits just inside the scalloped edge. Perfectly symmetrical and centred. It must stay **clear and recognisable when shrunk to 32 pixels**.
>
> **Colours:** champagne gold foil gradient (#8A6A35, #E9D39E, #B8935A, #F5E7BE) on deep onyx black (#0E0C0A). Also provide a version with a **wine-red (#5A1A24) wax seal** and a gold "A".
>
> **Style:** flat, crisp **vector-style** artwork with a subtle metallic gold gradient. No heavy 3D, no bevel or emboss clutter, no glow, no drop shadows, no textures, no photographic realism. Clean edges, generous spacing, minimal detail.
>
> **Avoid:** the letters "Au" or any chemical-symbol look, generic circle-with-initials marks, crowns, swirls, laurel wreaths, lions, diamonds, cartoon style, rainbow gradients, tiny details that disappear when small, and any extra words. If you include text, spell it **exactly**: AURUM (A-U-R-U-M).

## The set to ask for, one at a time (after you pick your favourite)
1. **Symbol only**, transparent background, 2048 × 2048.
2. **Symbol on onyx black** (#0E0C0A), square.
3. **Horizontal logo:** symbol on the left, the word **AURUM** on the right in widely letter-spaced high-contrast serif capitals, with a thin gold line under it and **INVOICE STUDIO** below in small, widely spaced sans-serif capitals.
4. **Stacked logo:** symbol on top, wordmark underneath, centred.
5. **App icon, 1024 × 1024:** a square on onyx black with the seal centred and sized to about **60% of the width**, so it isn't cut off when phones crop it to a circle or rounded square. No text.
6. **One-colour gold version** (flat gold, no gradient), transparent background.
7. **Black version** (flat black) for use on light backgrounds, transparent.

## Follow-up lines you can use
- *"Make the scalloped edge more subtle and elegant."*
- *"Make the A taller and thinner, with more contrast between thick and thin strokes."*
- *"Remove the star. Make it cleaner."*
- *"Show the envelope-flap idea more clearly in the crossbar."*
- *"Make the seal slightly larger inside the square, with less empty space."*

# TikTok ad 1: voiceover (hook A, about 27 seconds)

## 1. Paste this into ElevenLabs (with expression tags)

```
[curious, slightly shocked] Your customer asked "how much?"... and you sent THIS?

[dry, amused] No wonder they take three days to reply.

[short pause] [excited] Do this instead.

[lower voice, premium] Send a private link... sealed with your brand.

[smiling] They open it like a luxury delivery.

[impressed] And you see the exact moment they open it.

[confident, slow] Your business is premium. Your invoice should be too.

[warm, direct] Try it free. Three invoices a month. Link in my bio.
```

## 2. Test first (10 seconds, costs almost nothing)
Generate only line 1. If you hear the model READ the words in the brackets out loud, use the plain version below instead.

## 3. Plain version (no tags, expression comes from the punctuation)
```
Your customer asked "how much?"... and you sent THIS?

No wonder they take three days to reply.

Do this instead!

Send a private link... sealed with your brand.

They open it like a luxury delivery.

And you see the exact moment they open it.

Your business is premium. Your invoice should be too.

Try it free. Three invoices a month. Link in my bio.
```

## 4. Settings to start with (adjust to taste)
- Model: your newest (V4).
- Stability: low to medium (the lower it is, the more expressive and the more it changes between takes).
- Similarity: high.
- Speed: about 1.05 to 1.1 (TikTok likes brisk).
- Generate 3 to 4 takes of the whole script, then send me all of them. I pick the best line from each and cut them together.

## 5. Which voice to choose
Pick a voice that sounds like a confident friend talking, not a TV announcer:
- Young adult, warm, a little playful, clear Nigerian or West African English (this audience trusts it more than a foreign accent).
- Conversational and natural. Avoid "narrator", "documentary" or "news" voices.
- Female or male both work. Choose the one that sounds smiling when it says "Do this instead."
- If you already used a voice for the other two ads, keep the same one so all three ads sound like the same brand.
Audition each candidate with ONLY line 1 and line 5. The right one makes you smile at "luxury delivery".

## 6. Delivery notes (so the takes come out right)
- Line 1 is the hook. It must sound surprised, not angry.
- Line 2 is a dry joke. Slightly slower, a half smile.
- Line 3 is a quick, bright "turn". Almost a laugh.
- Lines 4 to 6 lower and warmer (this is the premium part).
- Line 7 is the slowest line. Let it breathe.
- Line 8 is quick and friendly, like telling a friend a secret.

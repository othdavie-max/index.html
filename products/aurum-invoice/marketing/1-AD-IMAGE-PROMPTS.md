# Aurum: 3 ad images (prompts for ChatGPT / Gemini)

## How to use these (read this first)
1. Open a **new chat** in ChatGPT (or Gemini). Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** first.
2. **Attach the reference images** listed under each ad (they're in the `references` folder).
3. Paste that ad's **PROMPT**. Ask for 4 variations, then pick the best.
4. **Add the text yourself in Canva.** AI image tools misspell words and prices, and a wrong price in an ad is a problem. The prompts below deliberately ask for NO text, and tell you exactly what to type and where.
5. Sizes: make **1080 × 1350** (4:5, for the Instagram/Facebook feed) and **1080 × 1920** (9:16, for Stories and Reels). Ask the tool to "make a vertical 9:16 version with the same scene" for the second.
6. In Stories, keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px, because the app covers those areas.

Fonts for your text in Canva: **Playfair Display** (headlines), **Manrope** (small text). Colours: gold `#D9BE84`, ivory `#F6F1E7`, onyx `#0E0C0A`.

Brand look: luxury **onyx black and champagne gold**, quiet, ceremonial and expensive, like Cartier, Hermès or Tom Ford. Materials: matte black cotton paper, gold foil, glossy wine-red wax seals with a scalloped edge. Lighting: soft warm key light from top left, gentle gold rim light, deep realistic shadows, a few tiny gold dust particles in the air. Photorealistic, 8k detail, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the product.

> **Strict rules:** do NOT add any text, letters, numbers, prices, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image. Leave clean, uncluttered empty areas exactly where I say, because I will add text later. No cartoon or illustration style. Where I attach reference images of the envelope, the wax seal or the phone screen, copy them exactly and do not invent new interface text. Keep the same envelope, seal and lighting in every image I ask for.

## If the AI gives you something wrong
- **Wrong envelope:** add *"Copy the envelope and wax seal exactly from the attached reference photo. Same proportions, same seal, same colours."*
- **Text appears in the image:** add *"Remove all text and lettering. The image must contain no words or numbers."*
- **Face looks fake:** add *"natural skin texture, candid, shot on an 85mm lens, no beauty filter."*
- **Too busy:** add *"Simplify. One subject, lots of empty dark space, minimal."*
- **Phone screen wrong:** attach `ref-02-envelope-opening.png` again and add *"The phone screen must be an exact copy of this attached screenshot."*

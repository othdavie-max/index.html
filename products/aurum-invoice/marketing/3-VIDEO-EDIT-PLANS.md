# Video plans: no presenter needed

Every video below uses only the clips in the `clips` folder, plus text on screen. A voice is optional.

## Quick CapCut guide (free app, phone or laptop)
1. New project, then **9:16**. Add the clips from the `clips` folder.
2. Cut where the plan says (CapCut: tap the clip, then **Split**; use **Speed** if a clip is too slow).
3. **Text:** add each line from the plan. Use the font **Playfair** or any elegant serif for headlines. White or gold text, a thin dark outline, and keep it inside the safe area (not in the top 250 px or bottom 340 px).
4. **Voice (optional):** Text, then **Text-to-speech**, pick a calm voice. Or record your own voice on your phone, which is usually more trusted. Never use a voice that pretends to be a customer.
5. **Captions:** Captions, then **Auto captions**, because most people watch with the sound off.
6. **Music:** use CapCut's free commercial-use library (soft, classy, instrumental). Don't use trending songs in ads, because you can lose the ad.
7. **Export:** 1080p, 30 fps.

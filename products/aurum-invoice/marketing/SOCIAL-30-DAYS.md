## 5. Grow without paying
1. **Comment on 10 posts a day** from Nigerian small businesses (real comments, not "nice"). People click on your profile.
2. **Reply to every comment and DM within the hour.**
3. **Share each post to your Story** with a "link" sticker to the website.
4. **WhatsApp Status:** post the 20-second video once a week. Your contacts are your first customers.
5. **Collaborate:** ask 3 businesses you know to try the demo and tag you.
6. **After each sale, ask for a review** (message below). Show real reviews, with permission, as posts.

## 6b. Things to avoid
- Don't post fake testimonials, or screenshots with sample names presented as customers.
- Don't promise tax, legal or "FIRS-approved" claims unless you have checked them yourself.
- Don't buy followers or "growth packages". They don't buy software.

## 7. Check what works

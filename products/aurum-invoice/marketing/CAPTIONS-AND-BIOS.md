## Rules that keep the page healthy
- Post the same video on TikTok, Reels and Shorts, but change the caption each time. Same text everywhere looks like spam.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. Replies feed the algorithm.
- When someone comments "how much?", reply with a short video. It is the best growth lever you have.
- Use 3 to 5 hashtags on TikTok and 5 to 8 on Instagram. More than that looks spammy.
- Do not promise results ("get paid in 24 hours"). Say what the app does: sealed link, open alerts, one-tap payment details.
- Never post a made-up customer reaction or review. Only post real ones with permission.

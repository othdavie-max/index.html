## 7. Backlinks you can earn for free
- **Guest posts:** offer a short, useful article (for example "5 ways to get paid on time") to Nigerian small business blogs, with a link to a relevant guide.
- **Local press and podcasts:** pitch a short story: "A Lagos builder made an invoice app that turns invoices into wax-sealed gifts."
- **Template sites:** ask sites that list free templates to link to your Excel templates.
- **Customers:** when a customer says something good, ask them to share it and tag you.

Privacy

This game has no accounts and no ads. Your saved games stay on your phone and never leave it.

To learn how the game is doing, it sends a few anonymous counts (for example "someone started a game", "someone reached year 2", "someone opened the World league"). These contain no name, no email, no location beyond the country and no cookies. If your browser has "Do Not Track" turned on, nothing is sent. Once the game is installed and you are offline, nothing is sent at all.

All countries, leaders and numbers are simulated and fictional characters are invented. This is a game, not a forecast.
